Australian Healthcare Workforce Analysis

Examining Workforce Growth, Distribution, and Sector Trends (2015–2024)

1. Introduction & The Big Picture

Australia faces ongoing challenges in maintaining a balanced healthcare workforce to keep pace with population growth and regional demands. This visualization project explores how individual health professions have evolved, scaled, and shifted relative to one another over a decade.

2. Growth by Profession (Streamgraph)

The streamgraph below illustrates the macro-level expansion and structural shifts across various Australian healthcare professions from 2015 to 2024. Symmetrical centering allows for clear observation of relative volume changes over time.

Tamara Munzner Framework Analysis

What (Data): Multi-year quantitative records capturing annual practitioner counts categorized by specific healthcare professions (e.g., Nurses and Midwives, Medical Practitioners, Psychologists).

Why (Task): To analyze trends over time, compare relative growth speeds across different health sectors, and identify shifts in workforce dominance.

How (Encoding & Idiom): An advanced Streamgraph idiom using stacked area marks with monotone interpolation for smooth curves. Quantitative years mapped to the X-axis, aggregated practitioner counts mapped to Y (centered stack layout), and nominal professions mapped to distinct categorical color channels.
